# ApexVoice Acceptable Use Policy, Draft

Provider: Harbour & Main, operating ApexVoice
Applies to: Business customers, their staff and contractors, and anyone using an ApexVoice account or number
Date of draft: November 28, 2026

## General rule

Use ApexVoice only for lawful business call handling for the business named on the account, in a way that is honest to callers, respects caller privacy, and does not put callers, the public, Harbour & Main, or other customers at risk. If a use is not clearly permitted, ask Harbour & Main before doing it.

## Prohibited uses

- Emergencies and safety critical services: do not use ApexVoice as the answering service for emergency lines, crisis lines, suicide prevention, poison control, alarm monitoring, or any service where a missed or misunderstood call could cause serious harm. Do not present ApexVoice as a way to reach emergency help.
- Impersonation and deception: do not configure the receptionist to claim to be human, to claim to be a named employee, to hide AI disclosure, to misrepresent the business, its prices, its credentials, or its availability, or to mislead callers about recording or transcription.
- Regulated advice: do not use ApexVoice to provide medical advice, diagnosis, triage, legal advice, financial advice, or any other regulated professional advice. See the Service Limitations document. Templates for regulated industries contain restrictions that must not be removed or worked around.
- Unlawful collection: do not collect personal information you have no lawful basis to collect, including health details, financial account numbers, passwords, government identification numbers, or information about children beyond what is needed to take a message for the business and lawful.
- Discrimination and harassment: do not configure the service to refuse service, treat callers differently, or screen callers on a protected ground under Canadian human rights law, and do not use call data to harass, threaten, or intimidate any person.
- Spam and unlawful marketing: do not use ApexVoice, its numbers, its messaging features, or its contact records to send commercial electronic messages that breach CASL, to make telemarketing calls that breach CRTC rules or the National Do Not Call List, or to send messages to a person who has withdrawn consent or unsubscribed.
- Fraud and crime: do not use the service for fraud, phishing, social engineering, impersonation of another business or a government body, debt or payment scams, or any other unlawful purpose.
- Surveillance: do not use the service to monitor employees, competitors, or private individuals, to record calls you are not a party to, or to intercept communications.
- Resale and sharing: do not resell, sublicense, share, or use one account to answer calls for a different business that is not named on the account and covered by the plan location and tenant limits, without written agreement from Harbour & Main.
- Security abuse: do not attempt to access another customer data, probe or bypass tenant isolation or authentication, extract system prompts or other customers configuration, overload the service, or introduce malicious code through configuration, knowledge content, or integrations.
- Data extraction and model abuse: do not use the service to harvest caller data for sale, to build a marketing list unrelated to the business callers contacted, to train a competing system on ApexVoice outputs at scale, or to generate content that breaches the law or another person rights.
- High volume misuse: do not use the service for robocalling, call flooding, directory harvesting, or traffic patterns that are not genuine inbound calls to the business. Harbour & Main may apply fair use limits where traffic is abusive or far outside the plan intended use, after notice where practical.
- Content in knowledge and instructions: do not add instructions that tell the receptionist to break this policy, to ignore caller objections, to refuse a human path, to keep secrets from the business own callers about how their information is used, or to produce unlawful, harassing, or deceptive responses.

## Business customer duties under this policy

- Keep configuration, greetings, knowledge, and instructions accurate, lawful, and consistent with this policy.
- Review templates before going live, especially for regulated industries, and keep the safety and privacy restrictions in place.
- Monitor messages and transcripts for misuse by your own staff, and remove staff access when it is no longer needed.
- Report suspected misuse, unauthorized access, or a caller complaint about the receptionist to Harbour & Main promptly.
- Cooperate with a Harbour & Main review of a suspected breach of this policy, including providing configuration history held by the business.

## Harbour & Main enforcement

- Harbour & Main may investigate a suspected breach using account, configuration, and usage records, respecting the Privacy Policy.
- Enforcement is proportionate and may include: a warning with a deadline to fix the issue, removal or correction of offending configuration or content, limits on features or traffic, suspension of a number or account, or termination of the subscription.
- Immediate suspension without prior notice is reserved for uses that create a legal, security, safety, or fraud risk, or that affect other customers or the public.
- A customer may respond to an enforcement decision by contacting support with the facts it wants considered. Harbour & Main will review a response in good faith, but a review does not require reinstating a use that breaches this policy or the law.
- Enforcement under this policy does not limit any other right under the Terms of Service or the law.

## Reporting misuse by others

Anyone who believes ApexVoice is being used in breach of this policy, including a caller who believes they were misled about AI, recording, or the use of their information, may contact Harbour & Main through the contact published with this policy. Reports should include the business name, the number called, the date and approximate time, and a short description. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report.
