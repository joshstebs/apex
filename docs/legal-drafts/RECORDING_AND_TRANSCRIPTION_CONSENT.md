# ApexVoice Recording and Transcription Consent, Draft

Provider: Harbour & Main, operating ApexVoice
Applies to: Business customers and callers to businesses that use ApexVoice
Date of draft: November 28, 2026

## What this document does

This document explains, in plain language, how transcription and recording work in ApexVoice, what callers are told, what agreement is relied on, and what choices a caller and a business have. It supports the Privacy Policy. It does not replace legal advice on consent in any specific province or sector.

## Current defaults

- Transcription: on. Calls handled by ApexVoice are converted to text so the business can review messages, see summaries, and follow up.
- Audio recording: not enabled by default. A call is not audio recorded for playback unless the business has enabled recording, the feature is available for its plan, and the caller disclosure for that business states that recording is on.
- Processing that is not a recording: telephony and AI providers process audio in real time to answer the call and create a transcript. Real time processing and any short lived technical buffering by providers must be described accurately in the final version of this document after the product team verifies provider behaviour. Do not tell callers that no audio is processed. Tell them whether an audio recording is kept.

## What callers are told

At the start of the call, before personal information is collected beyond the greeting, the receptionist states:

- That the caller is speaking with an AI receptionist for the named business.
- That the call is transcribed, and why: so the business can review the message and help the caller.
- If recording is enabled, that the call is recorded, and why.
- How to reach a human, or how a human callback is arranged.
- That the caller should not share detailed medical, legal, or other sensitive personal information on the call.

The exact scripts are in the AI Disclosure document. The script used must match the business configuration.

## Basis for transcription and recording

- Canada is generally described as one party consent for interception of private communications under the Criminal Code, meaning the consent of one party to the communication can make an interception lawful, subject to the exact statutory conditions. The business, acting through its configured receptionist, is a party to the call.
- Privacy law is separate. Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy laws, organizations need knowledge and consent for collection, use, and disclosure of personal information, and must identify purposes, subject to exceptions that require legal advice to rely on.
- ApexVoice therefore does not rely on one party consent alone. It uses upfront disclosure and the caller choice to continue, object, ask for a human, or use another contact method, as the transparency and consent approach, subject to legal confirmation before launch.
- Businesses must not use ApexVoice to record or transcribe calls in a setting where a different rule applies, for example certain health, legal, or financial contexts, or calls involving individuals in a jurisdiction with two party consent rules, without obtaining their own legal advice. Cross border callers are a real possibility for any published phone number.

## Caller choices

A caller may:

- Continue the call after disclosure.
- Ask for a human, and be transferred or receive a callback according to the business configuration.
- Object to transcription or recording, in which case the receptionist will offer the alternative contact method the business has configured and will note the objection on the call record.
- Hang up at any time. Hanging up ends collection from that call, other than the call metadata and any partial transcript already created, which are handled under the retention policy and any deletion request.
- Contact the business to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of their transcript or recording, as described in the Privacy Policy. The business will involve Harbour & Main as needed to locate and act on the record in that business tenant.

## Business customer choices and duties

- Choose, during onboarding, whether recording is on or off, where the feature is offered, and review that choice in the dashboard. The default is off.
- If recording is turned on or off, confirm that the caller greeting and disclosure have updated before taking live calls on the new setting.
- Set a retention period within the allowed range for transcripts and, if enabled, recordings, and tell callers and staff what it is if asked.
- Ensure staff understand that transcripts and recordings are personal information, must be accessed only for business purposes, and must not be downloaded, shared, or used for unrelated purposes such as staff monitoring beyond what the business has a lawful basis and a disclosed purpose to do. ApexVoice is sold as a customer call handling service, not as an employee surveillance tool.
- Do not use transcripts or recordings to make a decision about a caller that the caller would not reasonably expect from the disclosed purposes, without a separate lawful basis and, where needed, consent.

## Staff and internal calls

If a business own staff may be recorded or transcribed when calling its own number, the business is responsible for informing its staff. Harbour & Main does not provide employment advice.

## Retention and deletion of transcripts and recordings

Periods are set out in the Data Retention and Deletion document. In summary: recordings, where enabled, are kept for a short default period, transcripts are kept for the plan period, and both can be deleted earlier on a valid request or by the business in the dashboard, where that control exists, subject to legal holds and billing or dispute needs that are explained to the requester.

## Security

Transcripts and recordings are stored within the business tenant, access is limited by dashboard authentication and role, access and export are logged where the product supports it, and data is encrypted in transit. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

## Changes to this document

If Harbour & Main changes how transcription or recording works, for example a new transcription provider, a new default, or a new retention range, business customers will be notified before the change applies to live calls where practical, and caller scripts will be updated at the same time, not after.
