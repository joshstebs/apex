## Agreement

These terms govern the business customer use of ApexVoice, an AI receptionist service. By creating an account, configuring a receptionist, or using the service, the customer agrees to these terms on behalf of the business. The person accepting must have authority to bind the business.

If there is a signed agreement or order form between Harbour & Main and the customer that conflicts with these terms, the signed document governs to the extent of the conflict, subject to legal review of the final form.

## The service

- ApexVoice answers inbound calls to the customer telephone number or numbers assigned or connected through the service, using an AI receptionist configured by the customer.
- Features may include: a configured greeting and receptionist name, answers based on information the customer provides, message taking and delivery, appointment booking assistance where enabled and connected, call transfer according to customer rules, transcripts and AI generated summaries, a customer dashboard, and usage reporting.
- Features depend on the customer plan, configuration, and connected third party services. A feature shown in a demonstration using sample data is not a promise that the feature is live for the customer account unless it is listed for the customer plan and enabled in the customer dashboard.
- Harbour & Main may improve or change the service. Material changes that reduce a core feature the customer is paying for will be communicated in advance where practical, with options as described in the change section below.

## AI nature of the service, and service limitations

The customer acknowledges the Service Limitations document, which forms part of these terms. In summary:

- The receptionist is an AI system. It must be disclosed as AI to callers, and it must not be configured to claim to be human.
- AI systems can misunderstand callers, produce inaccurate summaries, and make booking or message errors. The customer must review messages, summaries, and bookings, and remains responsible for its own customer service decisions.
- The service is not for emergency calls and does not replace 911 or any emergency service.
- The service does not provide medical advice, legal advice, or any regulated professional advice, and must not be configured to do so.
- A booking, message, or notification is not confirmed until the connected system, for example a calendar or messaging provider, confirms it. The service will present confirmation status as reported by connected systems, and the customer must not treat an unconfirmed item as confirmed.

## Customer responsibilities

- Configuration accuracy: business name, hours, holiday closures, services, prices the customer chooses to share with callers, frequently asked questions, booking rules, transfer numbers, escalation contacts, and after hours behaviour. The receptionist can only be as accurate as the configuration and knowledge the customer provides and keeps current.
- Lawful instructions: the customer will not instruct the service to collect information it has no right to collect, to mislead callers, to hide AI disclosure, or to use caller information for a purpose the caller has not agreed to.
- Privacy: the customer is the organization collecting caller personal information for its purposes. The customer is responsible for its own privacy obligations to its callers and customers, including providing its own privacy information where required, handling caller access, correction, and deletion requests, and obtaining any consent it needs for follow up contact, marketing messages, or other uses beyond handling the call.
- Staff handling: the customer will ensure staff who receive messages, transcripts, and appointment information handle personal information appropriately, use it only for serving the caller and the customer business purposes, and protect account credentials.
- Telephone numbers and call forwarding: the customer is responsible for correctly configuring any forwarding from its own numbers and for any charges from its own carriers.
- Timely review: the customer will review messages and bookings within a time frame appropriate to its business, and will not rely on the service as its only channel for urgent matters.

## Harbour & Main responsibilities

- Provide the service with reasonable care, using the subprocessors disclosed in the Privacy Policy.
- Process caller personal information only on customer instructions given through the configuration and use of the service, except where the law requires otherwise, and as described in the Privacy Policy.
- Apply tenant isolation and access controls intended to keep each customer data separate from other customers.
- Provide service status and support through the channels listed for the customer plan.
- Notify the customer of a confirmed breach of security safeguards affecting its data, as required by law and as described in the Privacy Policy and the Data Retention and Deletion document.
- Support caller data access, correction, export, and deletion requests routed through the customer, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Accounts, users, and security

- The customer will provide accurate account information and keep it current.
- Each dashboard user must have their own account. The customer is responsible for activity under its accounts and for assigning roles appropriately, including limiting transcript and recording access.
- The customer will promptly remove access for staff who leave or change roles, and will notify Harbour & Main of suspected unauthorized access.
- Harbour & Main may suspend access where there is a credible security risk, with notice as soon as practical.
