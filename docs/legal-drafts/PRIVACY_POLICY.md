# ApexVoice Privacy Policy, Draft for Harbour & Main

Provider: Harbour & Main, operating ApexVoice
Applies to: Business customers, their authorized dashboard users, and callers to businesses that use ApexVoice
Date of draft: November 28, 2026
Privacy contact: To be completed by Harbour & Main before publication, name or role, email address, and mailing address in Canada. Do not publish this policy without a working privacy contact.

## Who we are and how this policy is organized

Harbour & Main provides ApexVoice, an AI receptionist service that businesses use to answer calls, take messages, answer configured questions, and help book appointments.

There are two kinds of personal information in this policy:

- Business customer information: information about the business that buys ApexVoice, its owners and staff who use the dashboard, its configuration, and its billing relationship with Harbour & Main.
- Caller information: information about individuals who call a business that uses ApexVoice, collected on behalf of that business.

For caller information, the business is the organization that decides why and how the information is collected and used. Harbour & Main processes caller information on the business instructions, through ApexVoice. A business may have its own privacy policy that also applies to its callers. If there is a conflict between a business instruction and this policy or applicable law, Harbour & Main will raise it with the business and will not follow an instruction that would breach applicable law.

## Personal information we collect

Business customer information:

- Business name, contact names, business email addresses, business phone numbers, and business address provided during onboarding.
- Configuration information the business provides: operating hours, services, pricing information the business chooses to publish to callers, frequently asked questions and answers, booking rules, transfer and escalation numbers, staff names and roles used for message routing, and business instructions for the receptionist.
- Dashboard account information: user names, email addresses, roles, login and security records, and audit logs of access to call data.
- Billing information: billing contact, plan, invoices, and payment status. Payment card information is processed by the payment processor and is not stored by Harbour & Main, subject to confirmation of the final billing setup before launch.
- Support communications with the business.

Caller information, collected on behalf of the business:

- Caller phone number and call metadata: date, time, duration, and the number called.
- Information the caller says during the call: name, contact details, message content, appointment requests, and any other information the caller chooses to share.
- A transcript of the call, where transcription is enabled, and an AI generated summary of the call.
- An audio recording of the call, only where the business has enabled recording and callers are told at the start of the call.
- Appointment and message records created from the call.

Callers should not share detailed medical information, legal information, financial account numbers, passwords, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information on a call with ApexVoice. The receptionist is configured to redirect callers who begin to share that kind of information.

## How personal information is used

Business customer information is used to:

- Provide, configure, and support ApexVoice for that business.
- Provide the dashboard, usage information, and service status.
- Bill for the service and manage the account.
- Communicate about the service, including security and service change notices.
- Improve reliability and safety, using de identified or aggregated information where possible.

Caller information is used, on the business instructions, to:

- Answer the call and respond to the caller request.
- Take and deliver a message to the business.
- Book, change, or cancel an appointment according to the business rules, where booking is enabled and confirmed by the connected booking system.
- Produce a transcript and summary for the business to review.
- Let the business follow up with the caller.

Harbour & Main does not sell personal information. Harbour & Main does not use caller information for advertising. Harbour & Main does not use business customer call content to train shared models by default, subject to verification of subprocessor terms described below, and any change to that position will be disclosed before it takes effect.

## Consent and disclosure to callers

- At the start of a call, the receptionist identifies itself as an AI receptionist for the business, states that the call is transcribed and, where applicable, recorded, and explains how to reach a human or use another contact method.
- By continuing the call after that disclosure, a caller is taken to agree to the transcription and, where disclosed, recording for the purposes stated, subject to applicable law and to legal review of this consent approach before launch.
- A caller may object, ask for a human, or contact the business by another method. Objections are recorded on the call record.
- The business is responsible for ensuring it has a lawful basis for collecting caller information through ApexVoice for its own purposes, including any follow up contact with the caller.

## Service providers and subprocessors

Harbour & Main uses service providers to operate ApexVoice. As of this draft, the categories and providers to be confirmed and listed publicly before launch are:

- Telephony: SignalWire, for inbound calling and related call data.
- Speech generation: ElevenLabs, for converting receptionist responses to spoken audio. Response text may include a caller name or appointment detail where needed for the conversation.
- Language model processing: a Hugging Face hosted model endpoint, for generating responses, summaries, and related processing of conversation content.
- Data storage and caching: Upstash Redis, for application data storage as configured.
- Hosting: Vercel, for application and dashboard hosting.
- Payment processing, email delivery, SMS delivery, error tracking, and analytics providers: to be named in the published version, with purpose and region for each.

Personal information may be processed or stored outside Canada, including in the United States, depending on provider and configuration. Harbour & Main remains accountable for information handled by its service providers and uses contractual and other measures intended to provide a comparable level of protection. The published version of this policy must list the actual regions in use, verified from live account configuration, and must not claim Canadian only storage unless that has been verified for every provider.

A maintained subprocessor list, with provider, purpose, data categories, and region, will be published and updated before material changes take effect where practical.

## Safeguards

- Access to caller information is limited by tenant: a business can access only its own calls, transcripts, messages, contacts, and appointments.
- Dashboard access requires authentication, and access to transcripts and recordings can be limited by user role.
- Data is encrypted in transit. Storage safeguards rely in part on provider encryption at rest, to be verified and described accurately in the published version.
- Audit logs record access to and export or deletion of caller information, subject to product implementation.
- No safeguard is perfect. Harbour & Main maintains an incident response process and will meet its legal notification duties if a breach of security safeguards occurs.

## Retention

Personal information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, the business instructions, and legal requirements. Recommended default retention periods are set out in the Data Retention and Deletion document, including transcript periods by plan, shorter periods for audio recordings where enabled, billing record retention required by law, and backup deletion timing. Businesses can see and, within policy limits, adjust retention for their tenant in the dashboard, where that control is built.

## Individual rights, access, correction, and deletion

- A caller who wants to access, correct, or delete personal information a business holds about them through ApexVoice should contact that business first. The business is the organization that collected it for its purposes.
- Harbour & Main will support the business in locating, correcting, exporting, or deleting caller information within the business tenant, within a reasonable time frame to be stated in the published version, subject to legal retention requirements such as billing records.
- A dashboard user or business contact may request access to or correction of their own information by contacting the privacy contact above.
- Requests may require identity verification. Some requests may be limited where the law permits or requires information to be kept.
- Complaints: contact the Harbour & Main privacy contact first. If a concern is not resolved, an individual may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and, where applicable, a provincial privacy regulator. Contact details to be added in the published version.

## Children

ApexVoice is a business service. It is not directed to children. If a child calls a business, the business is responsible for handling that information appropriately, and Harbour & Main will support deletion requests through the business as described above.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be communicated to business customers before they take effect where practical, and the published policy will show its effective date and a summary of material changes. Continued use of the service after a change takes effect will be treated as described in the Terms of Service, subject to legal review.
