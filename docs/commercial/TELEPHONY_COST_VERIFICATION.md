# SignalWire telephony cost verification

Checked 2026-11-28 against SignalWire's official pricing page (signalwire.com/pricing), Canada section, read in full on that date. Prices are USD list prices and change over time. No billable calls were placed for this verification, and no account invoice was accessed, so nothing here is invoice data. Items are labelled confirmed (published list price), code-verified (read in the ApexVoice codebase), or unverified (needs an invoice or account check).

## Confirmed published prices, Canada

* Inbound voice, local PSTN: $0.0066 per minute. Toll-free inbound: $0.0147 per minute.
* Outbound voice, local PSTN: $0.0080 per minute. Toll-free outbound: $0.0069 per minute.
* Telephone numbers: local DID $0.50 per month, toll-free $0.80 per month. Port-in is listed as free, port-out $5.00 per number.
* Cloud call recording: $0.002 per minute.
* Cloud call recording transcription: $0.020 per 15 seconds.
* Speech-to-Text: $0.015 per 15 seconds per use. Premium Speech-to-Text: $0.030 per 15 seconds per use.
* Call transfer by SIP Refer: $0.10 per refer. A transferred call also creates an outbound leg billed at outbound rates for its duration.
* Text to speech, per character, by tier: Economy $0.000008 (Amazon Polly standard voices, Google Standard and WaveNet, Azure standard legacy), Economy+ $0.000032 (Amazon Neural and Generative, Google Neural2), Standard $0.0001 (includes ElevenLabs Flash, Turbo and v3 Conversational served through SignalWire, Azure Neural, OpenAI tts-1, Deepgram Aura-1, Cartesia Sonic), Premium $0.00015 (Google Chirp 3 HD, Azure Neural HD, OpenAI tts-1-hd, Deepgram Aura-2), Pro $0.0002 (ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 and v3, Rime Coda).
* E911 service: $0.75 per month per number, when enabled.
* CNAM lookup $0.008 per lookup and number lookup $0.005 per lookup. ApexVoice does not use either today.
* Throughput: 1 call per second carries no monthly charge on the pricing page. Higher call-per-second tiers are billed monthly per tier and require a three month commitment. No general platform fee for Programmable Voice appears on the pricing page.

## Code-verified facts about how ApexVoice uses SignalWire

* The gather handler reads the SpeechResult parameter from the SignalWire webhook, so caller speech recognition is carrier gather speech. The codebase makes no call to the recording transcription API, and audio recording is off by default, so the recording and recording-transcription lines above are $0 in the current configuration.
* Speech output today is ElevenLabs audio generated by ApexVoice and played to the carrier, not SignalWire text to speech. Moving standard-tier tenants to SignalWire Say with an Economy+ voice is a backend change described in VOICE_COST_OPTIMIZATION.md and the integration patch spec.
* Outbound calling is disabled in code. Outbound rates matter only for call transfers, which create an outbound leg.

## Unverified, and why it matters

* Gather speech billing: the pricing page expresses Speech-to-Text as a price per 15 seconds per use, but how a Gather speech interaction maps to uses and segments is not stated on the page and cannot be settled from the code. Modelled in the pricing work as an allowance inside the audited other-lines figure of $0.031 CAD per minute. Worst case, if recognition were billed continuously for every minute of a call, it would add up to roughly $0.075 CAD per minute over that allowance and erase the standard tier's cost advantage. This is the single largest cost uncertainty and it can only be closed by reading a real SignalWire invoice for the existing Harbour & Main number after a month of representative test traffic, with Josh's approval to run that traffic.
* ElevenLabs billing track: the voice work found two published ElevenLabs rate tracks (subscription credit-effective cost versus a direct API list). Which one the ApexVoice account is actually billed on is unverified and changes the premium tier cost materially. Check the ElevenLabs account usage page or an invoice before restating premium pricing.
* Taxes, regulatory fees, and any account-specific discounts on the SignalWire account are not visible from the public pricing page.

## Updated profitability using verified prices

Voice figures are from VOICE_COST_OPTIMIZATION.md, at 425 spoken characters per call minute, FX 1 USD = 1.36 CAD, other lines held at the audited $0.031 CAD per minute (SignalWire transport at the confirmed $0.0066 USD inbound rate, the language model estimate, and the speech recognition allowance above), plus $7 CAD fixed cost per tenant per month in the margin table.

* Standard voice tier (SignalWire Say, Economy+ neural voice): total $0.0495 CAD per call minute. Gross margin at full use of included minutes: Starter $49 CAD with 120 minutes about 73.6 percent, Professional $99 with 350 minutes about 75.4 percent, Business $199 with 800 minutes about 76.6 percent.
* Premium voice tier (ElevenLabs Turbo at the audited credit-effective rate): total $0.135 CAD per call minute. Gross margin at full use: Starter about 52.6 percent, Professional about 45.2 percent, Business about 42.2 percent.
* Phrase caching at a modelled 30 percent repeatable share reduces synthesis cost further: standard about $0.0439 and premium about $0.1038 per call minute, with the caveat that carrier Say phrases must be pre-rendered to audio to benefit.
* Transfer-heavy tenants add $0.10 USD per SIP Refer plus outbound leg minutes. At a few transfers per month this is small, but it is a real per-event cost the current flat tiers absorb, so transfer volume should be metered per tenant from day one (the tenancy usage module already meters calls and minutes; transfer events should be added to its rates table during backend integration).

## Bottom line

* Confirmed: telephony transport and number rental are cheap and exactly as modelled in Phase 2. Text to speech through SignalWire is dramatically cheaper than direct ElevenLabs at list prices, which is what makes the under $0.07 CAD per minute standard tier real on published prices.
* Not confirmed: speech recognition billing on Gather, and the ElevenLabs account's actual rate track. Both are invoice checks, not research tasks, and both remain launch gates for paid tiers.
