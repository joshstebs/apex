Try the ApexVoice receptionist

SIMULATED: This demo runs in your browser. No real phone call is placed, nothing is booked, and no data leaves your browser.

This is the same conversation a caller has with ApexVoice, presented as a simulation so you can try it without a phone call. Pick an industry template inside the demo, change the sample business name and greeting, and try the scenario buttons: book an appointment request, ask about hours or prices, leave a message, or test an emergency.

What to try

  • Book an appointment. The receptionist collects a name, service, preferred time and phone number, then creates a request. In the live service the business confirms availability before anything is booked. The demo also lets you download a real calendar file for the request.
  • Leave a message. Notice the four fields it always collects: caller name, callback number, reason, and preferred contact time. Those are the fields you see in the live dashboard.
  • Ask about price or hours. Answers come only from the sample template data, and the replies say so. The live service answers only from information the business provides.
  • Say something urgent. Emergency wording gets a call 911 response, not a booking.
  • Listen. If your browser supports it, Listen buttons play a browser voice preview. The live phone service uses a different voice, and the demo labels it that way.
