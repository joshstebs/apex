## Long description

ApexVoice answers your business phone when you are on a job, with a customer, or closed for the day. You connect it by forwarding your existing business number when you are busy or closed, or by using a new local number provisioned for the service. During a guided setup you enter your hours and holidays, the services and prices you want quoted, your answers to common questions, and how you want messages delivered. Eight industry templates give you a starting point written for your kind of business.

On each call, the receptionist introduces itself as an AI receptionist for your business, answers questions from your information, and takes a structured message. It can also take an appointment request, which is never a confirmed booking until you confirm availability. Messages, requests and call transcripts appear in your business dashboard.

ApexVoice is built for Canadian businesses. Callers are told their words may be transcribed so you can follow up, audio recording is off by default, and privacy practices are based on PIPEDA. The receptionist speaks English today. It does not give medical, legal or financial advice, it never asks callers for payment card numbers, banking details, passwords or health card numbers, and it directs emergencies to 911.

## FAQ copy

### Does ApexVoice book appointments?
It takes appointment requests. It collects the service, a preferred time and a callback number, and the request goes to the business. Nothing is confirmed to the caller until the business confirms availability, so a caller is never promised a slot the business cannot offer.

### What happens in an emergency?
Callers who describe an emergency, for example a gas smell, chest pain or severe bleeding, are told to call 911 right away, and the receptionist does not try to book or troubleshoot. Urgent but non-emergency keywords the business sets, such as no heat or flooding, follow its escalation rules: transfer to its number, or an urgent message flagged for the owner.

### What if the receptionist cannot answer a question?
It answers only from information the business provides: services, prices, hours and the business's own answers to common questions. If a question is outside that information, it says so and offers to take a message so the business can answer it directly. It does not guess at prices, policies or availability.

### What languages does it speak?
English today.

### Do I have to change my phone number?
No. Most businesses forward their existing number to ApexVoice when they are busy, closed, or unable to answer. A new local number is available if the business prefers to keep the two separate.

### What does the business receive after a call?
A message in the dashboard with the caller's name, callback number, reason for calling and preferred contact time, plus the call transcript. Appointment requests appear as requests waiting for confirmation. Delivery by email or text depends on the plan and settings.
