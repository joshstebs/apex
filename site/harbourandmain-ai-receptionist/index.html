ApexVoice: an AI receptionist for Canadian small businesses

ApexVoice answers your business phone when you cannot. It greets callers, answers common questions from information you provide, takes messages, and takes appointment requests that you confirm. You see every message in a simple dashboard.

It is built for owner-operated businesses that lose calls while they are on a job, with a customer, or closed for the day: trades, repair shops, clinics, salons, restaurants, agencies and professional offices.

How it works

  • Connect your number. Forward your existing business number to ApexVoice when you are busy or closed, or use a new local number we provision for the service. You choose when it answers.
  • Tell it about your business. In a guided setup you enter your hours and holidays, services and prices you want quoted, answers to common questions, and how you want messages delivered. Industry templates give you a starting point written for your kind of business.
  • It answers and takes the details. On each call it introduces itself as an AI receptionist, answers questions from your information, and takes a message with the caller's name, callback number, reason for calling and preferred contact time. It can also take an appointment request.
  • You stay in control of bookings. An appointment request is never a confirmed booking. You confirm availability first, then the customer is confirmed.
  • You get the message. Messages and requests appear in your business dashboard, and can also be delivered by email or text, depending on your plan and settings.

Features

  • Answers inbound calls during the hours you set, including after hours and holidays if you choose
  • Greets callers with your business name and a receptionist name you choose
  • Answers common questions using only the services, prices, hours and answers you provide. It does not invent prices or policies
  • Takes structured messages: caller name, callback number, reason, and preferred contact time
  • Takes appointment requests that you confirm before anything is booked
  • Transfer and escalation rules you set, for example urgent keywords that route to a transfer number or an urgent message
  • Business dashboard with call history, messages, appointment requests and usage
  • Eight industry templates with greetings, common questions and safety rules written for each industry
  • Canadian privacy posture: PIPEDA based practices, callers told the call may be transcribed, audio recording off by default
  • Priced in Canadian dollars

ApexVoice speaks English today. It is a receptionist, not a replacement for your judgement: it does not give medical, legal or financial advice, and it directs emergencies to 911.

AI and privacy, in plain language

  • Callers know it is an AI. The receptionist introduces itself as an AI (virtual) receptionist for your business at the start of the call. It never pretends to be a person.
  • Transcription is disclosed. Callers are told their words may be transcribed so the business can follow up. The transcript is what appears in your dashboard.
  • Audio recording is off by default. ApexVoice does not record call audio unless a business turns recording on and the disclosure wording is updated to say so.
  • Canadian privacy law. The service is operated under Canadian privacy practices based on PIPEDA. Message details are collected for the business that received the call, so it can return the call or follow up on a request.
  • Sensitive information is out of bounds. The receptionist does not ask for payment card numbers, banking details, passwords, health card numbers or other sensitive identifiers, and it does not give medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Emergencies. ApexVoice is not an emergency service. Callers describing an emergency are told to call 911.

Common questions

Does ApexVoice book appointments?

It takes appointment requests. It collects the service, a preferred time and a callback number, and the request goes to you. Nothing is confirmed to the caller until you confirm availability. That way a caller is never promised a slot you cannot offer.

What happens in an emergency?

Callers who describe an emergency, for example a gas smell, chest pain or severe bleeding, are told to call 911 right away, and the receptionist does not try to book or troubleshoot. Urgent but non-emergency keywords you set, such as no heat or flooding, follow your escalation rules: transfer to your number, or an urgent message flagged for you.

What if the receptionist cannot answer a question?

It answers only from the information you provide: your services, prices, hours and your own answers to common questions. If a question is outside that information, it says so and offers to take a message so you can answer it yourself. It does not guess at prices, policies or availability.

What languages does it speak?

English today. If your callers regularly need another language, tell us in the demo request so we understand the demand.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Most businesses forward their existing number to ApexVoice when they are busy, closed, or unable to answer. A new local number is available if you prefer to keep the two separate.

What exactly do I receive after a call?

A message in your dashboard with the caller's name, callback number, reason for calling and preferred contact time, plus the call transcript. Appointment requests are shown as requests waiting for your confirmation. Delivery by email or text depends on your plan and settings.

Is my callers' information used to train AI models or shared?

Messages and transcripts are collected so your business can follow up with the caller. They are not sold. Our privacy practices are described in the AI and privacy section above, and full policy documents are provided to customers before an account goes live.
